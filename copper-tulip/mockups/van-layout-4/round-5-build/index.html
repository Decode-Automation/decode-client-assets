Flower bar FAQs

What happens after I request a date?

We reach out to talk color, inspiration and flower requests, then send a 50% deposit invoice. Pay within 7 days to hold your date.

How far ahead should I book?

At least a week before your event.

Is there a minimum?

Yes. Our event minimum is $600.

Can the party run longer than two hours?

Yes. Extra time is $75 an hour.

We have more than 30 guests. Can you do it?

Yes. We’ll put together a custom quote.

Can I add vases or flower carriers?

Yes. Vases are $5 each and ribboned flower carriers are $4 each.
